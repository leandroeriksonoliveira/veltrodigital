Notas Inteligentes

Ambient intelligence platform that records the session, transcribes with AI and generates structured notes in the professional's format — SOAP for physicians, IRAC/ILAC for lawyers and other templates.

The client's pain point

Liberal professionals lose time typing medical records and meeting notes after appointments. Manual documentation delays the schedule, causes fatigue and increases the risk of losing clinical or legal information.

How we arrived at the solution

01

Discovery

Understanding the real appointment flow and note formats required by each field.

02

Scoping

Template definition, privacy, plans and integration with the professional's daily workflow.

03

Development

SaaS product with recording, STT, note generation and export — focused on reducing administrative burden.

What the system does

  • Session recording and transcription (live and post-appointment)
  • AI-structured notes with profession-specific templates
  • Export to Word and PDF
  • Web access, no local installation required

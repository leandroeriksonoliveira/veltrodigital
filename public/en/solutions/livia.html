LivIA

Voice assistant for seniors, families and operations — digital inclusion focused on senior autonomy and peace of mind for those monitoring from a distance.

The client's pain point

Many seniors struggle with complex apps. Families want to monitor well-being and routine without turning the phone into a barrier. Generic "smart home" solutions don't address the real emotional and operational context.

How we arrived at the solution

01

Discovery

Interviews and usage mapping with seniors, family members and caregivers.

02

Scoping

Voice interaction, alerts, roles and interface complexity limits.

03

Development

App + backend + admin ecosystem, prioritizing simplicity and trust — not excessive features.

What the solution delivers

  • Voice interaction designed for seniors
  • Family monitoring with clear roles
  • Mobile + cloud architecture for continuous operation
  • Focus on digital inclusion and autonomy

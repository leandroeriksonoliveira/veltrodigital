Featured · Digital Compliance

Digital Compliance Report

Complete audit of your website against LGPD, Marco Civil da Internet, CDC and your profession's regulatory bodies — CFM, OAB, CREA, CAU and other applicable councils.

Non-compliant websites may face penalties already provided by law: ANPD fines (up to 2% of revenue, capped at R$ 50 million per violation), public warnings, data blocking and ethical-disciplinary sanctions from professional councils.

  • Technical and legal checklist with risk prioritization
  • LGPD, Marco Civil and CFM, OAB and sector regulator standards
  • Action plan with recommended fixes and suggested timelines

Advisory report for digital risk management. Does not replace individualized legal counsel.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to deliver a website? +
Average delivery time is 10 to 20 business days, depending on project complexity.
What is included in the services? +
Custom design, development, WhatsApp integration, SEO, technical support, content updates and performance reports via Google Analytics 4.
Do websites follow CFM and OAB standards? +
Yes. Projects for physicians are built in accordance with CFM Resolution 2.336/2023 guidelines, and for lawyers they respect OAB Provision 205/2021. Data handling in compliance with LGPD.
What is the Digital Compliance Report? +
It is an audit of your website or social media profile that checks adherence to LGPD, Marco Civil da Internet, CDC and your profession's regulatory body (CFM, OAB, etc.). Use the online analyzer to receive an immediate numeric score. The detailed diagnosis stays with our team to guide corrections and avoid penalties already provided by law — such as ANPD fines and disciplinary sanctions.
What technology is used? +
We use the best technologies on the market, such as Next.js and React, ensuring maximum performance, excellent Core Web Vitals scores and ultra-fast loading.

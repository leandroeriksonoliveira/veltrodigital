Digital Compliance

Website and social media audit against LGPD, Marco Civil da Internet, CDC and professional regulatory body standards (CFM, OAB and other councils).

The client's pain point

Professionals and companies publish content and collect data without knowing if they're exposed to ANPD fines, disciplinary sanctions or reputational risk. An objective, prioritized diagnosis is missing.

How we arrived at the solution

01

Discovery

Sector checklist (healthcare, legal, etc.) and mapping of what the client publishes online.

02

Scoping

Compliance criteria, scoreboard and risk prioritization.

03

Development

Online analyzer with numeric report and commercial follow-up for corrections.

What the solution delivers

  • Numeric score by category (LGPD, Marco Civil, professional ethics)
  • Visual risk seal and summary of possible penalties
  • Online analyzer from social media link and/or website
  • Correction guidance with the Veltro team (does not replace legal counsel)

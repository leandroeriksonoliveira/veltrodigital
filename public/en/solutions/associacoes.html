Association Platforms

Digital solutions for entities and associations — such as APADEFI and other institutional presence and management projects — designed from the organization's real processes.

The client's pain point

Associations accumulate manual processes, fragmented communication with members and websites that don't reflect their institutional role. Without a system aligned to operations, management loses time and clarity.

How we arrived at the solution

01

Discovery

Meetings with leadership and operations to map members, communication and priorities.

02

Scoping

Business case, platform scope and success criteria aligned with the entity's mission.

03

Development

Custom delivery: digital presence, internal workflows and channels that will actually be used.

What these solutions cover

  • Institutional presence and member communication
  • Management workflows adapted to the entity's reality
  • Proposal and scoping before code
  • Phased evolution without technological excess
